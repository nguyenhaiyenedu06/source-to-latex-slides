Retrieved and modified 2026-10-22. Modifications: removed logo/campus images and visible institutional branding; removed Chinese-font dependency; added configurable font, language strings and artwork; adapted cover spacing for 16:9; added a citation helper and neutral contents/closing layouts. The section navigation, colored title bars and three-part footer are retained. No upstream photos or logo assets are distributed.

`main.tex` is a bilingual layout/font specimen, not an evidence-reviewed academic deck. Empty artwork paths permit template checks without generating arbitrary images. A real presentation must replace specimen text, select topic-specific colors/artwork, cite its provided sources, and pass independent evidence and visual review.
